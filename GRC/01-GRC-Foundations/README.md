# GRC Foundations – Hands-On Lab

## Overview

Completed the Beginner GRC Foundations Lab on Do GRC to develop practical knowledge of Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) concepts.

The lab covered foundational GRC activities including risk identification, security policies, control objectives, and communicating security risks in a business context.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) fundamentals
- Risk identification
- Risk statement development
- Business impact considerations
- Security control concepts
- Policy and governance concepts
- Risk communication
- Basic GRC analysis

## Practical Work

During the lab, I completed multiple scenario-based exercises and assessments covering:

- Identifying and describing cybersecurity risks
- Connecting security weaknesses to potential business impacts
- Understanding control objectives
- Applying basic GRC terminology
- Evaluating security scenarios from a risk perspective
- Answering practical GRC assessment questions
